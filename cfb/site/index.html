Every source is graded on the same FBS-vs-FBS games: week 2 on, bowls and playoff games included, and only games all three projected. MAE is how many points the projected home margin missed the final margin by, on average. Log-loss scores the home team's win probability; lower is better, a coin flip scores 0.693, and ties are left out. The better of RAPPA and ESPN is in bold. The closing market (consensus spread for margin, de-vigged consensus moneyline for win probability) is the reference both are chasing. RAPPA − ESPN is the per-game difference with its standard error; negative (green) means RAPPA was better. RAPPA rates quarterbacks on their own (following transfers) and uses each game's starter, and adds special teams (punts and kickoffs, valued by expected points and counted at face value) to its scrimmage ratings. Its win probability is Φ(margin / σ), with σ the spread of its own earlier misses. 2022 is the warm-up season: the first with ratings, and so no prior. Built by uv run -m cfb.benchmark.

Composite Offense Defense Plays
Click any column to sort; click again to reverse. Quarterbacks are rated on their own: offense Pass is the rest of the passing game, and QB is the team's passers' rating weighted by their dropbacks. Composites blend rush and pass (with QB) at the league play mix; net = off + def. Ratings are neutral-site PPA per play vs the average FBS team, from each season's full fit, shrunk toward the season before. Every FCS opponent is rated as one pooled team, which is not shown. Power conferences are the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12 and ACC, the Pac-12 through 2023, and Notre Dame.
